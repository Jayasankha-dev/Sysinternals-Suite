Security Hardening & Prevention

The best investigation is the one an organization never needs to perform. Hardening reduces attack surface and increases the cost of successful intrusion.

17.1 Windows

  • Maintain security updates.
  • Use least privilege and application control.
  • Enable modern endpoint protection and tamper resistance.
  • Protect administrative accounts.
  • Collect high-value telemetry.

17.2 Android

  • Keep devices and apps patched.
  • Use strong device authentication.
  • Review application permissions.
  • Use managed-device controls for enterprise fleets.

17.3 Network

  • Segment sensitive systems.
  • Monitor egress traffic.
  • Use secure DNS and appropriate filtering.
  • Restrict administrative protocols.

17.4 People and Process

Security awareness, incident playbooks, access reviews, vendor risk management, and tested backups are technical controls' essential partners.
