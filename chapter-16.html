Module 16 · Chapter 16

Enterprise EDR, SIEM & Security Operations

Enterprise defense depends on collecting signals from endpoints, identities, cloud services, applications, and networks, then turning them into prioritized investigations.

16.1 EDR

Endpoint Detection and Response products collect endpoint telemetry and provide investigation and response capabilities. Their effectiveness depends on sensor health, configuration, coverage, and analyst interpretation.

16.2 SIEM

A SIEM correlates logs from many sources. Good normalization and asset identity are critical to producing useful investigations.

16.3 SOC Workflow

  1. Alert triage.
  2. Enrichment.
  3. Scope assessment.
  4. Containment decision.
  5. Evidence collection.
  6. Remediation.
  7. Post-incident review.

16.4 Purple Teaming

Defenders can validate detections through controlled simulations in authorized environments. Measure whether expected telemetry appeared and whether analysts could investigate it.
