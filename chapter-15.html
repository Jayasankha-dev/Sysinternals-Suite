Module 15 · Chapter 15

Advanced Incident Response & Digital Forensics

Incident response is a controlled process for understanding, containing, eradicating, and learning from security events.

15.1 Preparation

Maintain logging, asset inventories, escalation contacts, backup strategies, forensic procedures, and tested recovery plans before an incident occurs.

15.2 Identification

Validate the alert, establish scope, identify affected accounts and assets, and create a preliminary timeline.

15.3 Containment

Choose short-term and long-term containment appropriate to the incident. Consider business impact, evidence preservation, and the possibility of attacker lateral movement.

15.4 Eradication and Recovery

Remove persistence, remediate vulnerabilities, reset compromised credentials where required, restore trusted systems, and monitor closely.

15.5 Lessons Learned

Determine why controls failed, which telemetry was missing, and what engineering changes will reduce recurrence.

Golden rule: containment is not the same as eradication. A disconnected endpoint can still contain evidence of compromise or persistence.
